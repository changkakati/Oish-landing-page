Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Oish is a personal finance tracking application designed to help users record and manage their income and spending. Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Oish.

2. Information We Collect

Oish may collect information that you provide when creating and using your account, such as your username and authentication information.

The app may also store financial entries that you intentionally add, such as income, spending amounts, categories, payment methods, and dates.

3. How We Use Information

Information stored by Oish is used to provide the core functionality of the application, including displaying your income, spending, and financial summaries.

We do not use your financial entries for advertising.

4. Data Storage

Information associated with your Oish account may be stored on servers used to provide the application. Appropriate measures are taken to protect stored information from unauthorized access.

5. Data Sharing

Oish does not sell your personal information or financial information to third parties.

Information may only be shared when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, or protect the security and rights of the service and its users.

6. Advertising and Purchases

Oish currently does not contain advertisements or in-app purchases.

7. Account and Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of your Oish account or associated data, please contact us using the support email below.

8. Children's Privacy

Oish is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
